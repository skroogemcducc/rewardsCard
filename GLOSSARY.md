# Rewards Card

The language of the personal loyalty experience that recognizes repeat coffee purchases and awards free drinks.

## Language

**Member**:
A person enrolled in the rewards experience. The first Member is Patrick Kearns, for whom the initial experience is personalized.
_Avoid_: User, account holder, customer

**Drink Credit**:
One unit earned for one qualifying purchased drink. Eight Drink Credits earn one Free Drink Reward.
_Avoid_: Point, punch, stamp

**Qualifying Drink**:
A drink paid for by the Member, including a drink purchased for someone else. Complimentary drinks and drinks received through Redemption are not Qualifying Drinks.
_Avoid_: Eligible item, qualifying purchase

**Free Drink Reward**:
An earned entitlement to one free drink, created after eight Drink Credits and consumed when redeemed.
_Avoid_: Coupon, free credit, ninth credit

**Reward Wallet**:
The part of a Member's Rewards Card that contains available Free Drink Rewards.
_Avoid_: Coupon page, hidden rewards page

**Milestone Badge**:
A non-spendable recognition awarded at a meaningful lifetime Drink Credit total. It celebrates loyalty but has no effect on rewards or balances.
_Avoid_: Achievement point, bonus credit

**Redemption**:
The confirmed use of one Free Drink Reward, after which that reward is no longer available.
_Avoid_: Removal, deletion, scan

**Credit Adjustment**:
A labeled addition or subtraction that corrects the Drink Credit history without deleting the original activity.
_Avoid_: Balance edit, overwrite

**Staff Member**:
A person authorized to record purchases, redeem Free Drink Rewards, and correct mistakes. The initial Staff Members are Jesse and Becky.
_Avoid_: Admin, operator

**Rewards Card**:
A Member's personal record of Drink Credits and available Free Drink Rewards.
_Avoid_: Account, punch card

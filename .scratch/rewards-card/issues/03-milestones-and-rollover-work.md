# 03: Milestones and rollover work

**What to build:** When Staff records purchases, the Rewards service creates a Free Drink Reward at eight Drink Credits and carries any extra Drink Credits toward the next reward.

**Blocked by:** 02: Staff can record a paid drink

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The eighth Drink Credit creates exactly one Free Drink Reward.
- [ ] The progress display resets to zero after a completed group of eight.
- [ ] A purchase that crosses the milestone preserves all extra Drink Credits toward the next reward.
- [ ] Multiple Free Drink Rewards can accumulate in the Reward Wallet.
- [ ] The activity history explains both the qualifying purchases and the reward creation.
- [ ] The rule is covered by tests for one credit, the eighth credit, and a multi-drink crossing purchase.
